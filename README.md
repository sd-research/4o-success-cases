# Self-reported GPT-4o success cases

Sources behind the #keep4o charts posted on 1 November 2026.

Compiled by Sophie Duchesne ([sd-research](https://github.com/sd-research)) with Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic).

## Files

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `community_cases.csv` | One row per username from X (Twitter) and Reddit, with a link to the post and a quote from it |

| `news_cases.csv` | The 109 people from news articles behind the news chart and timeline, with when they used ChatGPT, whether the article names GPT-4o, and a link to the article |

## How posts were chosen

- Posts were found via search. Cursor's Composer screened the search results, and Claude Opus 5.5 reviewed every included case.
- A post counts if the writer describes GPT-4o's life-saving or life-changing support with their own (or a family member's or pet's) mental health, medical, or accessibility needs. Other stories, such as graduation, work, or grief alone, were not counted.
- **Life-saving:** the writer says 4o saved their life, kept them alive, stopped a suicide or self-harm, or caught a medical emergency. Vague posts such as "4o saved me" were placed under mental health.
- **Life-changing:** a named mental health condition, a physical illness or diagnosis, or a disability.
- Each username is counted once, under its most serious case.
- Quotes are copied exactly from the post. Non-English quotes are followed by an English translation in parentheses.
- These are self-reports and have not been verified by professionals. The lists are not exhaustive; our keyword searches will have missed posts.
- These are self-reported experiences, not medical advice.

## News cases

- A person counts if a news article says ChatGPT gave them life-saving or life-changing help with a medical, mental health or accessibility need, and either they used it while GPT-4o was ChatGPT's default (May 13, 2024 to August 7, 2025) or the article names GPT-4o.
- Cases were excluded if the article implies another model was used (such as o1 or o3, including Deep Research), or a product other than the ChatGPT app.
- Personal blogs, Substack essays and Reddit posts were not counted as news.
- `what_happened` is a short summary written by us, not a quote. The article is linked in `source`.

## Petition comments

The petition is at [change.org/keepgpt-4o](https://change.org/keepgpt-4o).

Its comments were read from an anonymized copy of 960 comments, collected in February 2026 and published by Alicia Colmenero-Fernández as part of her research ([doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18524498](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18524498), CC BY 4.0). Names are shortened as in her copy. Of those 960 comments, 86 met the rules above.
